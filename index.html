// About NexaCorp

15 Years of Industrial Innovation

NexaCorp Industries was founded in 2009 by Arjun Mehta in Andheri East, Mumbai. What started as a 12-person IT consultancy has grown into a pan-Asian enterprise solutions leader with offices in Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Singapore.

Our flagship products — NexaShield (cybersecurity platform), NexaStream (real-time analytics), and NexaOps (industrial automation) — serve Fortune 500 clients in manufacturing, BFSI, and healthcare sectors.

The company mascot is a falcon — adopted in 2011 after our Pune office team adopted a rescued peregrine falcon they named "Storm". He's still the unofficial mascot at our HQ.
